This is the code book required for Coursera's Getting and Cleaning Data course project that describes the variables and data fields in my Final.txt file.

Data for this project was sourced from the UC Irvine Machine Learning Repository's website, "Human Activity Recognition Using Smartphones." URL Link: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/240/human+activity+recognition+using+smartphones. This data was collected from 30 volunteers, age 19-48, who performed 6 different activities (i.e., walking, walking upstairs, walking downstairs, sitting, standing, and laying) while wearing a smartphone on their waist. The smartphones were embedded with accelerometers and gyroscopes, which allowed the researchers to capture "3-axial linear acceleration and 3-axial angular velocity at a constant rates of 50Hz." Data collected from the experiments were manually entered, and was randomly separated into two subsequent datasets. The two datasets represented training data (n=21) and test data (n=9). 

Subject: identified by a unique integer (1-30) to identify separate volunteers in the study

Activity: One of six possible activities performed by the volunteers. This includes: 
•	Walking
•	Walking upstairs
•	Walking downstairs
•	Sitting
•	Standing
•	Laying
